How is urgency decided?
Fixed safety rules, not a diagnosis. RED: any danger sign, breathlessness, pregnancy problem, injury, or temp 39.5°C+. AMBER: temp 38.5°C+, child under 5 with fever or diarrhoea, age 65+ with 2 or more symptoms, or 3 or more symptoms. Otherwise GREEN. A doctor reviews every case and can override the level.
